It's a Dog's World

This version uses Tencent's real Hunyuan3D-2.1 browser Space for image-to-3D generation. Upload your dog drawing inside the generator, use background removal for white paper, and generate the textured 3D asset there.

Real model: Hunyuan3D-2.1
Input: dog drawing / white paper
Output: 3D mesh + texture
How to use

1. Upload your dog drawing in the Hunyuan panel.

2. Turn on Remove Background for drawings photographed on white paper.

3. Use the full generation option when you want texture, not just shape.

4. Download the GLB when it finishes. That GLB is what we'll later drop into your globe and give a safe idle.

Stored only in this browser. Not sent to Hunyuan.
Open Hunyuan full screen
The Tencent Space runs on shared Hugging Face GPU capacity, so it can occasionally queue, sleep, or restart.
Next step after generation works reliably: automatically capture the returned GLB and place it into your dog globe with an idle chosen from the model's geometry.
