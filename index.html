Use the real Microsoft TRELLIS.2 image-to-3D generator directly in this browser. Upload your dog drawing, generate the textured 3D asset, and export the GLB when you like the result.

Real model: Microsoft TRELLIS.2
Works on: phone / tablet / desktop
Output: textured GLB
For your drawings

1. Upload the dog drawing in the TRELLIS panel.

2. White paper is okay — TRELLIS.2 preprocesses the image and its pipeline includes background removal.

3. Start at 512 resolution on mobile for faster testing.

4. Tap Generate, inspect the 3D result, then Extract GLB.

5. If the first shape drifts from your drawing, regenerate with another seed instead of “fixing” the drawing.
